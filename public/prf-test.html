PRF parity — web baseline

Mints an iCloud-Keychain passkey on braird.app, reads its WebAuthn PRF for a fixed eval salt, and builds a prf-v1 unwrap blob. Compare the PRF hex against the native iOS harness (must be byte-identical), then unwrap the blob with the native PRF to recover the test master key.
